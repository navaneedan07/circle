Privacy

The short version: Circle has nowhere to send your data, because it has no server. Everything below is what the software actually does, not an aspiration.

What this application collects

Circle has no account system, no sign-in, and no analytics. It does not collect telemetry, usage statistics, device identifiers, IP addresses or crash reports. There is no mechanism in the application for transmitting the contents of your archive to us, because the project has no server-side component to receive it.

The website that hosts this page is ordinary static hosting. Like any web server, the host may keep standard access logs (IP address, user agent, requested URL). Those are the host's logs, not Circle's, and they contain nothing about your archive.

What stays on your machine

Circle stores everything it derives from your exports locally:

What Where it lives
Imported messages, contacts and derived relationships A SQLite database, Documents/Circle/circle.db
Copied media such as voice notes Documents/Circle/media
Your folder choice and app settings Documents/Circle/settings.json
Your original export files Exactly where you already keep them. Circle reads them in place and never modifies, moves or deletes them.

The model runs locally

Circle answers questions using Ollama running on your own computer. Your questions and the excerpts used to answer them are sent to that local process over your own machine's loopback interface. They are not sent to a remote service, and the model provider does not receive them.

The one thing that does leave your machine

Installing the prerequisites downloads software from the internet: the Ollama installer from ollama.com, and model weights from the Ollama library. Those downloads tell those servers your IP address, as any download does. Nothing about your archive is included in them — only the request for the software.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Circle removes the application. To remove your data as well, delete the Documents/Circle folder. Because nothing was ever copied anywhere else, that is the whole deletion — there is no remote copy, no backup, and no retention period to wait out. Your original export files are untouched and remain wherever you keep them.

Children

Circle is a general-purpose personal archive tool and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address and the date below will change with it. Because the application collects nothing, a change here cannot affect data already on your machine.
