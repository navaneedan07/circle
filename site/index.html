Circle
a private record

Your messages already know who matters. Circle helps you ask.

Point Circle at the exports you already have — WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram, email, calendar, voice notes. It reads them, works out who is who, and answers questions like “who do I talk to most” or “what did we promise about the project”. Everything runs on your own machine.

Download for Windows macOS — soon Linux — soon

One file, about 105 MB. Nothing to install, no account, no server.

Setting it up

  1. Install Ollama and leave it running. It is the only other thing Circle needs, and it is what lets answers be generated on your own hardware.
  2. Pull the model, in a terminal:
    ollama pull gemma3:4b
    ollama pull nomic-embed-text
  3. Unzip Circle and double-click Circle.exe. Your browser opens automatically.
  4. Drop your exports into the folder Circle watches. It picks them up on its own.

First run indexes everything and can take a few minutes. The Settings screen shows progress and tells you if a model is missing.

What Circle reads

  • Chat exports: WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram, X
  • Email (mbox and individual messages)
  • Calendar (ICS)
  • Voice notes and other attachments inside exports
  • Notes and documents you drop in

What Circle does not do

  • It does not connect to any social account. It reads files.
  • It never merges two people without asking you first.
  • It does not send anything anywhere, because there is nowhere for it to send it.

Where your data lives

In one archive file inside Documents\Circle\circle.db, on this machine. Nothing is uploaded, synced or backed up anywhere you did not ask for. Close Circle and it is gone; delete that folder and so is everything in it.
